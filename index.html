Frostbow Showdown

Pick your archer. Drag back, aim, let go. Walk with the arrow buttons to line up a shot or dodge. Knock down the rival's tower for bonus damage. Beat levels to unlock new arrows.

How to play

  • Shoot. Drag back anywhere on the screen, aim with the dotted guide, and let go. Pull further for more power.
  • Win. A headshot does 35 damage and a body hit does 20. Knock the rival's health to zero before they do it to you.
  • Beat the clock. Level 1 gives you 10 minutes. Every level takes a minute off, down to 1 minute on level 10. The music speeds up as time runs out.
  • Walk and dodge. Hold the two arrow buttons to walk on your go, or to dodge while the rival's arrow is in the air. The blue bar is your stamina.
  • Towers. Hitting a tower knocks out blocks. Each lost block drops the archer and costs them 10 health. Stronger arrows smash towers faster.
  • Special arrows. Flame burns for 2 turns. Freeze makes the rival miss a go. Poison hurts for 3 turns. Boom explodes. Beat levels to unlock them.
  • Wind. The arrows at the top show which way the wind pushes your shot.
  • Bow workshop. Every win earns coins. Spend them on a longer aim guide, more range and faster arrows. Beat level 10 to win the Golden Bow.
  • Keep it on your phone. iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen or Install app. After the first visit it works offline.
